Why SwapX

Built for Serious Traders

Every feature engineered for speed, security, and a seamless trading experience — without compromising on decentralization.

Instant Escrow Settlement

Funds are held in smart-contract escrow the moment a trade begins. Release is instant and automatic upon payment confirmation — no delays, no disputes.

Zero Platform Fees

0%

We never take a cut. Traders set their own spreads. Only network gas costs apply.

Military-Grade Security

Non-custodial architecture means your keys, your coins. Multi-sig wallets, 2FA, and biometric auth built in.

2-Min Settlements

The fastest P2P settlement on the market. Automated dispute resolution handles edge cases in under 15 minutes.

250+ Payment Methods

Bank transfers, mobile money, PayPal, cash, and local payment rails — accept what works for you and your counterparty.

Privacy by Default

Trade pseudonymously. KYC is optional and tiered by volume. Your financial data stays yours — always encrypted, never sold.
